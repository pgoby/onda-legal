1. In short

  • Onda is for adults (18+) only.
  • On Onda you upload your own photo, get a 1–10 first-impression score from other people and see your average; in return you score other people's photos. Others see your photo only if you upload it.
  • People who score you are anonymous; you cannot see who scored you, and they cannot see your scores. Your results are shown only to you.
  • Person check when you add a photo (with your explicit consent, on your phone only): we compare the photo with a quick live selfie on your phone to check that the person in it is you. The selfie and face data never leave your phone and are never saved; we only receive the result. If you don't consent, your photo is still added and stays hidden until a person on our team has checked it.
  • The "Verified" badge (blue check) is optional; a person reviews your selfie and the selfie is deleted 30 days after the decision.
  • A few AI-generated profiles clearly labelled "Sample" may appear in the feed; they are not real people and never score anyone.
  • No ads, no sale of data, no cross-app tracking.
  • To improve the app, pseudonymous usage statistics go to PostHog servers in the EU; they never contain your name, email, photos or scores. Turn them off in Settings → Privacy → "Share usage data".
  • Your data is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). You can delete your account in the app at any time.

3. On-device checks when you add a photo

When you add a photo to the feed or to the Premium comparison, the app runs two checks only on your phone to stop profiles built with someone else's photos and nude photos.

Person check (with your explicit consent)

  • You take a quick live selfie with the front camera; you cannot pick one from your library.
  • Your phone computes a numeric face descriptor (128 numbers) for the selfie and for the faces in the photo you add, and compares them. Such a descriptor may count as biometric data under KVKK art. 6 and GDPR art. 9, so we ask for your explicit consent before the first check.
  • The selfie and descriptors never leave your phone and are never saved. They are kept in memory for at most 10 minutes for other photos you add in the same session, then deleted; they are deleted right away when you sign out.
  • Only the result reaches our server: match / no match / no face in the photo / check not possible, a similarity range (e.g. "strong", "borderline"; not a score or a descriptor) and the model version.
  • A photo that does not match, or is added without a selfie, stays hidden until a person on our team has checked it.

Nudity check

Your phone runs a classifier on the photo. If nudity is likely, the photo is not uploaded; if it is uncertain, the photo stays hidden until our team has checked it. Only the result range (low / medium) reaches the server. This check does not use face data.

Your consent

Your decision on face comparison is stored with the version of the consent text and the time (as proof). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy → "Face comparison"; the selfie in memory is then deleted and your new photos go live after review by our team. Not consenting never disables a feature.

Models used

Face comparison and the nudity check use open-source models that run on your phone. To find the face in the frame, the on-device Google ML Kit face detection library is used. Your photo or selfie is never sent to any third party for these checks. Under Google's terms, the ML Kit library may send Google technical performance and usage metrics about how the app uses the library; these metrics contain no photos, selfies or face data.

4. Optional "Verified" badge

  • If you ask for the badge, the app asks for a front-camera selfie doing a randomly chosen pose (e.g. "thumbs up with your right hand"); you cannot pick it from your library. Your phone only checks that there is exactly one face in the picture (on the device, nothing is kept).
  • The selfie is uploaded to a private store that only authorised moderators can open and is reviewed by a person against your profile photos. It is never shown to other users.
  • We keep: the selfie file, the requested pose, the request date, the decision (approved / rejected), the rejection reason and the reviewer.
  • The selfie is deleted 30 days after the decision, or right away if you delete your account. The badge (the date you were verified) stays while your account exists and shows as a blue check on your cards.
  • The badge review uses no automated face recognition or biometric matching. Every feature works without the badge.

5. Sample profiles

While the community is small, you may see a few AI-generated profiles in the feed that are clearly labelled "Sample". These profiles:

  • are not real people and do not represent any real person;
  • never score anyone, never like or message anyone, and never affect your results or anyone else's;
  • if an average is shown for them, it is computed only from scores given by real users;
  • are removed from the feed as the number of real users grows.

A score you give to a sample profile is processed as your vote record like any other vote.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. Only these providers, acting on our behalf and instructions (as processors), are used to run the service:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage, error logs): servers in Frankfurt (EU).
  • RevenueCat and Apple (subscriptions and payment): subscription status and account ID. Apple takes the payment; your card details never reach us.
  • Expo and the Apple Push Notification service (sending notifications): push token and notification text.
  • PostHog (product analytics): EU cloud, servers in Frankfurt. Only the pseudonymous usage data listed below; nothing is sent if you turn it off.
  • Google ML Kit (face detection library running on your phone): only the technical metrics described above; no photos or face data.
  • Public authorities and courts with legal power to request data, only where legally required.

We do not use an external provider for photo moderation; if we start to, this policy will be updated beforehand.

Other users only see your approved photo, the information you allow to be shown (default: your initial) and, if you have it, the blue check. Only you see the details of your scores and results.

8. International transfers

Your data is stored on servers in the EU (Frankfurt); under Turkish law this is a transfer abroad. Subscription (RevenueCat, Apple), notification (Expo, Apple) and analytics (PostHog) providers or their sub-processors may process data outside the EU, for example in the USA. These transfers fall under KVKK art. 9 and GDPR Chapter V; they rely on safeguards such as the providers' data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses. Write to us for more information.

10. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Your account, profile, photos, verification selfie and feedback files are deleted. Votes you gave are also removed from other people's averages (by a nightly job over a few nights, to avoid sudden jumps). Deletion cannot be undone.

  • Subscription: deleting your account does not stop your subscription; cancel it separately in your Apple account (section 13).
  • Sign in with Apple: if you signed in with Apple, you can also remove Onda's access on your iPhone under Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple.

11. Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 you can: learn whether your personal data is processed; request information if it is; learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties it is transferred to at home or abroad; ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected; ask for deletion or destruction under KVKK art. 7; ask for corrections and deletions to be notified to recipients; object to a result against you that arises solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Under the GDPR you can: access, rectify and erase your data, restrict processing, object, receive your data (portability), withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

How to apply: write to cagatycaglr38@gmail.com, preferably from the email address registered in the app. We may ask for additional information to verify your identity. We reply free of charge within 30 days at the latest. You may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board or, in the EU, to the data protection authority in your country. You can also view, correct and delete some of your data in the app.

12. People under 18

Onda is not for anyone under 18. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and re-check it on the server; an under-18 entry locks the account. Accounts and content we identify as belonging to a minor are removed immediately. Photos of children are prohibited. Details: Child Safety Policy.

13. Subscriptions

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription purchased through the Apple App Store; your payment details never reach us. To cancel: on iPhone, Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Details: Terms of Use.

14. Moderation, reporting and security

  • Photos get an on-device person and nudity check when you add them; photos that don't match, are added without a selfie or look doubtful stay hidden until a person on our team has checked them. Our team may also review photos before and after they go live.
  • Every card has Report and Hide / Block. Reports are reviewed by a person within 24 hours; violations lead to content removal, restriction or closure of the account. You can appeal a decision at cagatycaglr38@gmail.com.
  • Where content showing the sexual abuse of children is found, it is removed, the account is closed and the case is reported to the competent authorities as required by applicable law (Child Safety Policy).
  • Screenshots are blocked in the feed and on other people's profiles.
  • Your data is encrypted in transit; access is role-based and only authorised moderators can open verification selfies. No system is 100% secure; if a data breach occurs we make the notifications required by law.

15. Tracking, cookies and product analytics

Onda is a mobile app and does not use cookies. It does not track you for advertising, does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not combine your data with data from other companies' apps or websites and does not share it with data brokers. These web pages do not use cookies, external scripts or trackers either; GitHub, which hosts them, may keep technical access logs under its own privacy policy.

Product analytics (PostHog, EU cloud). To understand which parts of the app are used and where people get stuck, we collect pseudonymous usage events:

  • What is sent: the pattern of the screen opened (IDs are masked as :id); feature events with small labels: app opened, sign-up step and completion, photo uploaded (angle), vote cast (a count only, never the score you gave), paywall viewed (where it was opened from), purchase started / completed / failed (plan), report sent (reason), invite shared (type). Also the app version, operating system and version, language and time zone.
  • What is never sent: your name, email, photos, scores given or received, contacts, date of birth, city or any finer location, device name. IP-based location is off and IP addresses are not stored. There is no session replay.
  • Identity: your account ID is never sent to PostHog. Either an irreversible hash (SHA-256) of your account ID salted with a secret value, or a random ID generated by the app, is used.
  • Turning it off: Settings → Privacy → "Share usage data". When it is off, no usage data is sent from this device and the random device ID is reset; turning it back on starts with a new ID.
