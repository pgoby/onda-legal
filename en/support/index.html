Support

Having a problem or a question? Have a look at the questions below; if you can't find the answer, write to us.

Support email: nefesalap@gmail.com

If you mention the email address registered in the app and describe the problem briefly, we can help faster. We will never ask for your password or one-time code; do not share them.

Frequently asked questions

How does Onda work?

You add your own photo; once it has been checked and approved, it is shown to other people in the feed. Others give your photo a 1–10 first-impression score, and you score other people's photos. You see your average and score distribution on your profile; Premium adds the grouped profile of your raters, age and city breakdown, trend, weekly summary and a two-photo comparison. Nobody sees who scored whom.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your account, profile, photos and data are deleted, and the scores you gave are removed from other people's averages within a few nights. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app, write to nefesalap@gmail.com from your registered email address and we will delete your account.

Note: deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it separately using the steps below.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple; we cannot cancel them from inside the app.

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions and choose Onda.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period; Premium stays on until then. To avoid being charged for the renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Apple takes the payments and also handles refunds: you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Premium but it isn't active

Tap Restore purchases on the paywall or in Profile → Settings. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple account. If the problem continues, write to us.

How do I report a photo or a person?
  1. Tap the "…" menu on the card in the feed and choose Report.
  2. Pick a reason: Fake profile, Someone else's photo, Under 18, Nudity, Harassment or Other.
  3. Add a short note if you like and tap Send. You can then block the person if you want.

Your report is anonymous; the person you report does not see who reported them and disappears from your feed right away. Reports are reviewed by a person within 24 hours; under-18 reports go to the front of the queue. If there is a violation, the content is removed and, where needed, the account is closed.

Someone is using my photo

Report the card with the reason Someone else's photo. If you also write to nefesalap@gmail.com (with a short explanation showing that the photo is yours), we review it with priority.

I want to block someone or stop seeing them

From the card's "…" menu you can choose not to see the person again, and you can block them after reporting. You and a person you block will not see each other anywhere in the app; they are not told. With Hide me from my contacts in Settings you can avoid being shown to people in your contacts at all.

Why is my photo "In review"?

If the on-device check when you added the photo did not find a match, you continued without a selfie, or the photo looked doubtful, it stays hidden until a person on our team has checked it. After the review your photo goes live or we tell you why it didn't.

What does face comparison do, and how do I turn it off?

When you add a photo, it compares a quick live selfie with the person in the photo on your phone only; the selfie and face data never leave your phone and are never saved. It works only with your explicit consent. Turn it off in Settings → Privacy → Face comparison; while it is off, your new photos go live after approval by our team. Details: Privacy Policy.

How do I get the blue check?

In Profile → Settings, choose to get the Verified badge, take a front-camera selfie doing the requested pose and send it. A person compares your selfie with your profile photos; you get the result as a notification. The badge is optional; the selfie is deleted 30 days after the decision.

What are profiles labelled "Sample"?

While the community is small, you may see a few AI-generated profiles in the feed, clearly marked with a "Sample" label. They are not real people; they never score anyone and never affect anyone's results. They are removed as the number of real users grows.

How do I turn off usage data sharing?

Turn off Settings → Privacy → Share usage data. These pseudonymous statistics never contain your name, email, photos or scores.

How do I get a copy of my data or have it deleted?

For requests under KVKK and the GDPR, write to nefesalap@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days at the latest. Details: Privacy Policy.

I am worried about a child's safety

Report the card with the reason Under 18 and write to nefesalap@gmail.com. Do not download, screenshot or send the suspicious content to anyone. If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first (112 in Türkiye and the EU). Details: Child Safety Policy.
