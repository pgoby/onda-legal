1. What Onda is

Onda is an app where adults voluntarily get a 1–10 first-impression score on their own photos from other users and, in return, score other users' photos. Everyone who receives scores has joined by uploading their own photo and accepting these rules. Only you see your average and results; Premium offers a detailed analysis.

Scores are personal impressions; they do not measure your worth or beauty.

This version has no matching, messaging or chat features. If such a feature is added in the future, its rules will be added to these terms before it becomes available.

Sample profiles: while the community is small, a few AI-generated profiles clearly labelled "Sample" may appear in the feed. They are not real people; they never score, like or message anyone and never affect any user's results. Any average shown for them is computed only from real users' scores. They are removed as the number of real users grows.

2. Who can use Onda

  • You must be at least 18 years old. You must enter your real date of birth; a false statement is grounds for closing your account.
  • You must not be legally barred from using the app. If an account of yours was previously closed for violating the rules, do not open a new one.
  • You are responsible for your account; do not share your sign-in details.

3. Account and the "Verified" badge

The "Verified" badge (blue check) is optional. If you want it, you take a selfie doing the pose we ask for and a person on our team compares it with your profile photos. If approved, a blue check shows on your profile and cards; if not, we tell you why and you can try again (at most 3 requests in 24 hours). The selfie is used only for verification and is deleted 30 days after the decision. The badge is not an absolute guarantee of who someone is; if you see an account using someone else's photos, report it.

4. Community rules

The following are prohibited on Onda:

  • Use by or content of minors: people under 18 may not use Onda; photos may not show children, and photos showing children are removed. Any content that sexualises or exploits minors is removed immediately, the account is closed and the content is reported to the competent authorities.
  • Other people's photos: upload only photos of yourself in which your own face is shown. Photos of celebrities, acquaintances or photos found online are prohibited.
  • Fake photos: filters that change your face beyond recognition, AI-generated or AI-altered photos.
  • Nudity, sexual or obscene content.
  • Bullying and harassment: targeting, humiliating or threatening a person, hate speech, discrimination; using photos or scores to shame or expose users or spreading them through screenshots.
  • Sharing other people's personal data without permission, spam, fraud.
  • Manipulation: fake accounts, bots, coordinated or fraudulent scoring, buying or selling votes.
  • Any illegal content or conduct.

Be respectful: scoring means sharing an impression of a person. Be honest, not cruel.

5. Scores, averages and fairness

Averages are computed from the scores users give. Averages of photos with few votes are pulled toward the overall mean, and suspicious or fraudulent votes may be down-weighted or not counted. To protect privacy, small groups are not shown and results are computed with a delay. We do not guarantee the accuracy of scores or any particular result.

7. Reporting and moderation

  • Every card has Report and Hide / Block. Report any violation you see; the person you report disappears from your feed right away.
  • Reports are reviewed by a person within 24 hours.
  • When a violation is confirmed, the content is removed. Depending on how serious it is, we issue a warning, restrict the account temporarily or close the account permanently. An account is closed after three confirmed violations; for serious violations such as use by a minor or content that sexualises minors, it is closed the first time.
  • You can appeal a decision at cagatycaglr38@gmail.com; appeals are reviewed by a person.
  • Photos may go through on-device checks and review by our team before and after they go live. We cannot guarantee that every violation will be caught, which is why your reports matter.
