Onda has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Onda is for adults

  • Onda is only for people aged 18 and over. We ask for a date of birth at sign-up and re-check it on the server; an under-18 entry locks the account.
  • Photos may not show children; only photos showing the user themselves are accepted.
  • This version has no messaging, chat or comment features between users.

2. Strictly prohibited

  • Any image, text or link that shows the sexual abuse of children or sexualises children (CSAM).
  • Any behaviour aimed at contacting, deceiving or exploiting minors for sexual purposes (e.g. grooming, sextortion).
  • Accounts opened by anyone under 18, or photos of anyone under 18.

3. Our safeguards

  • Age gate: date-of-birth check; an under-18 entry locks the account.
  • Checks before upload: photos go through an on-device nudity check when they are added; explicit content is not uploaded and uncertain content stays hidden until our team has checked it. The person check makes it harder to upload someone else's photo.
  • Human review: doubtful photos are reviewed by our team before they go live.
  • Reporting: every card has Report, and the "Under 18" reason goes to the front of the queue.
  • Screenshot blocking: screenshots are blocked in the feed and on other people's profiles.

4. How to report

  • In the app: on the card, tap "…" → Report → Under 18.
  • Email: cagatycaglr38@gmail.com. Briefly describe the user's display name, when you saw it and what happened.
  • Directly to the authorities: in Türkiye via İhbar Web (ihbarweb.org.tr) or to the police or public prosecutor; internationally to NCMEC's CyberTipline, or to your local police.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first (112 in Türkiye and the EU).

Important: do not download, screenshot, keep or send suspicious content to anyone (including us). Possessing or sharing such content is a crime. Just describe where you saw it and whose account it was.

5. What we do when we receive a report

  • We review child safety reports with priority, by a person, and always within 24 hours.
  • We remove the content immediately.
  • We permanently close accounts belonging to minors and accounts that upload content sexualising or exploiting children.
  • Where content showing the sexual abuse of children is found, we report it to the competent authorities as required by applicable law, and keep the records needed for an investigation only to the extent and for as long as the law requires.
  • We cooperate with lawful requests from the competent authorities.
