Onda: First Impression

Find out what first impression your photo makes. Upload your own photo, get an honest 1–10 first-impression score from other people, and score other people's photos in return.

  • Adults (18+) only. Everyone joins voluntarily, with their own photo.
  • Anonymous: nobody sees who scored whom; your results are shown only to you.
  • Safety: photos are checked on the phone when they are added, every card has Report and Block, and reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
  • Privacy: no ads, no sale of data; data is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt).

The app is called "Onda: İlk İzlenim" on the App Store.
